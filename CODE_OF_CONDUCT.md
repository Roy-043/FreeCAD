# FreeCAD Project Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is designed to promote a welcoming, positive and constructive environment for all participants in the FreeCAD project. By adhering to these guidelines, we can maintain a respectful, open community for collaboration, development, and support.

## 1. Community Guidelines

- **Be respectful and considerate**: Treat everyone with respect, kindness, and empathy. Avoid personal attacks, discriminatory comments, and any form of harassment.
- **Be constructive**: When providing feedback, do so in a constructive manner. Recognize that the source code and other contributions are collectively owned. Avoid assigning blame to individuals, focus on issues instead.
- **Be willing to collaborate**: Welcome individuals from diverse backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. Be open to different approaches and ideas. If in any way possible, try to contribute to the project.

## 2. Forum Guidelines

The FreeCAD forum is governed by the Community Guidelines. But the following more detailed guidelines also apply.

- **Do not post inappropriate content**:
  - Do not post copyrighted material unless you are the copyrightholder.
  - Do not post spam. Messages whose main purpose is to promote a product or website are not allowed.
  - You can use AI to translate texts, but do not post AI generated content that adds nothing to a topic or discussion.
  - Do not attack other community members.
  - Do not use foul language, do not swear or insult.
- **Always stay constructive**: Provide additional information or test files when requested. If applicable, follow the bug reporting guidelines as published on the forum. Be patient and acccept that your problem may not be solved right away.
- **Do not ecscalate conflicts**: If conflicts arise, do not add fuel but instead de-escalate. If you think a forum member misbehaves or you feel injustly treated, report the problem to moderators so that they can take appropriate actions.
- **Use GitHub for discussions related to the FreeCAD source code**: We use GitHub to develop the FreeCAD software and therefore coding related discussions happen there. If you want to participate in those discussions, or contribute to the software, you must use that platform.

## 3. Moderator Guidelines
- **Moderation Responsibilities**:
  - Monitor discussions for any breaches of the code of conduct, and take appropriate action as outlined below.
  - Remove inappropriate content. A moderator must remove posts, comments, or images that violate the rules. This includes off-topic material that derails the conversation.
  - Keep discussions on-topic. If a conversation strays too far from its original purpose, a moderator should gently guide it back on track to keep it relevant and productive.
  - Organize content. A moderator may merge duplicate discussion threads, split a single conversation into multiple topics if it becomes too broad so users can easily find what they are looking for.
  - De-escalate conflicts. When debates become heated and unproductive, a moderator may issue a public reminder to remain civil, temporarily lock a thread, or warn individuals. Threads that remain unproductive may be permanently locked.
  - Manage users. In cases of repeated or severe rule-breaking, a moderator has the authority to warn, suspend, or permanently ban users from the community.
- **Responding to Violations**: If a guideline is violated, moderators should address the issue promptly. This may involve reminding participants of the code of conduct or issuing warnings.
- **Communication**: Moderators should maintain transparent communication when enforcing guidelines, explaining actions taken to the community while respecting involved parties' privacy.
- **Impartiality**: Handle disputes and violations without favoritism. Treat all community members equally, regardless of their status or contributions.

## 4. Enforcement
- **Reporting Violations**: Community members should report violations of this code of conduct to moderators. Reports can be made via the forum or directly through established communication channels.
- **Preliminary Investigation**: Upon receiving a report, moderators will conduct a preliminary investigation. This may involve reaching out to involved parties for further information.
- **Consequences**:
  - **Warnings**: For minor infractions, moderators may issue up to two warnings to the offending party.
  - **Temporary Suspension**: For repeated minor infractions or a single severe breach, a temporary suspension from the community may be enforced.
  - **Permanent Ban**: In cases of severe misconduct, including harassment or significant disruption, a permanent ban from the community may be warranted.
- **Appeals Process**: Community members have the right to appeal moderation decisions. Appeals should be directed to the moderation team. The appeals will be reviewed, and a final decision will be communicated.

## 5. Conclusion
We are committed to creating a safe, welcoming, and productive environment for all community members involved with FreeCAD. By adhering to this code of conduct, we can ensure everyone can contribute positively and thrive in our community.

## Amendments
This code of conduct may be amended periodically to reflect changes in community standards or operational needs. Community input will be considered when making amendments.
